Why this is the honest star of the whole system

Guessing up or down intraday hits a hard ceiling (~52–54%) — proven every which way. But guessing how big the next move will be is a genuinely easier, more predictable problem: volatility clusters. That's why this model lands ~68% on BankNifty and ~74% on Nifty50 out-of-sample — real, not inflated.

How you trade it (direction-free): when it says BIG MOVE, you buy a straddle — you win whichever way it breaks, as long as it moves enough. When it says QUIET, you sell premium with a defined-risk iron condor and collect time-decay while price stays in the range.

Honest limits: ~68–74% still misses ~1 in 4. It says a big move is likely, not exactly how big or when. Options cost spread + theta — size small, never bet the account.
